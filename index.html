      Settings

      Ovulation is estimated as your expected next period minus this many days. 14 is the usual average, and it can range from about 10 to 16 days between people. The fertile window runs from 5 days before ovulation to 1 day after, because sperm can survive up to about 5 days and the egg lasts roughly a day.

      Backup

      Your entries stay in this browser only. Download a backup file and keep it in your own storage, such as Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Restore it any time.

      Other ways

      Dates are estimates from the calendar method. They cannot confirm ovulation and are not a method of contraception. See a doctor for irregular cycles or any concern.
